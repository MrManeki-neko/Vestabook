# Vestabook Remote — User Manual

Control the split-flap board from your phone. One page, that's all you need.

## Get it on your phone (one time)

**iPhone (Safari):** open **https://vestabook.vercel.app**, tap the **Share** button
(square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. A "Vestabook" icon
appears on your home screen — use that from now on.

**Android (Chrome):** open the same address, tap the **⋮** menu, then
**Add to Home screen** (or "Install app").

**First launch:** tap **Settings** (gear icon) and enter the **secret code** — ask Matt
for it. You only do this once; the app remembers it.

## What you're looking at

- The big dark grid is a **live copy of what's on the board right now**.
- Under it: which **book** is playing and how far along it is (e.g. "Frame 214 of 4,880").
- The board turns the page **every 5 minutes**, all by itself.

## The controls

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **A book's name** | The board switches to that book and plays it on repeat. |
| **Shuffle** | When one book finishes, a random one starts. |
| **Auto-cycle** | The default: plays every book in order, forever. |
| **Pause** | Freezes the board where it is. Nothing is lost. |
| **Resume** | Picks up with the very next passage — exactly where it left off. |
| **Page turn: 5 / 10 min** | How often the board turns the page. The story continues from the same spot. |

## The two golden rules

1. **Changes take about 30 seconds.** After you tap a button the app says "Applying…" —
   that's normal. Don't mash the button; give it half a minute.
2. **A quiet board at night is not broken.** The board sleeps on its do-not-disturb
   schedule (you'll see a "Quiet hours" badge). It wakes up on its own and continues
   where the story left off.

## If something looks wrong

- **Board hasn't changed in a while?** Check for a **Paused** or **Quiet hours** badge
  in the app. If neither is showing and it's still stuck, tell Matt.
- **"Unauthorized" message?** The secret code is missing or mistyped — re-enter it in
  Settings.
- Nothing you tap can break anything. Worst case, tap **Resume** and **Auto-cycle** and
  the board goes back to its normal routine.
